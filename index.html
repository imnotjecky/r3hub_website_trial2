About Us

Building Smarter IT Infrastructure for a Connected Future

R3Hub Technologies is a Davao–Cebu based company providing IT solutions and services all over the Philippines. We were founded by experienced professionals with over a decade in IT infrastructure, engineering, system integration, and software management systems.

Our expertise covers data centers, power and cooling, network security, healthcare systems, ERP, auxiliary systems, and other IT-related solutions. The name R3HUB reflects our commitment to high-quality services and solutions.

  • 10+ years of combined experience
  • Offices in Davao and Cebu
  • Certified support engineers
  • Nationwide project delivery
